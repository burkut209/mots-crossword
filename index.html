A moment to settle in

Choose a clue, then write or type in its highlighted squares. Tap a crossing square again to switch between Across and Down.

Apple Pencil

Enable Settings → Apple Pencil → Scribble on your iPad. Use Pencil mode and write directly in a square; iPadOS turns handwriting into letters. Add the puzzle languages to your iPad keyboards if recognition needs help. Use Focus + before writing for larger squares.

Pencil mode keeps your place while you write. It uses Apple’s handwriting recognition; handwriting accuracy depends on your device and language settings. Finger handwriting is not included.

Keyboard & corrections

Choose Keyboard and tap a square. Letters move you along the word. Arrow keys move through the grid; Space switches direction at a crossing. Backspace deletes. You can also type or paste a whole answer starting at a selected square.

Erase square and Undo correct entries. Check word marks entered letters only when you ask. A filled puzzle is not automatically marked correct. Revealed squares count as help.

Languages & spelling

Clues use the answer’s language. Translate clue changes only the clue text. French accents and Spanish vowel accents are omitted from answers; Spanish Ñ and Turkish İ, I, Ç, Ğ, Ö, Ş, Ü are preserved. Spaces are omitted; Turkish Â, Î, Û become A, İ, U. In a Turkish word, lowercase i becomes İ and ı becomes I.

Offline & privacy

After “Ready offline” appears, Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen makes this feel like an app. Open it once from the Home Screen while online. Your puzzle and progress stay in this browser on this device. Clearing website data removes them. No account or payment is needed to play.

All puzzles, translations, and checking run locally. The hosting provider may keep ordinary web access logs. This first edition has a curated word bank, so words can recur across puzzles.

Remove your data

You can erase all saved puzzles, nicknames, settings, and downloaded offline files for Mots in this browser. Export first if you want a backup. Removing local data does not delete the hosted website.
